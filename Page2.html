The Expanded History of Aqueduct

Early History

Early aqueducts were used to water crops, especially in the Fertile Crescent. The Minoan Civilization on Crete may have been what inspired the Romans to use them.

The Roman Empire

As their empire expanded, the Roman Empire used aqueducts in their cities, from Britain to the Middle East. Rome itself had over 200 miles of aqueducts throughout the city.

Modern Age

America boasts the largest aqueducts in the Modern World. The Catskill Aqueduct carries water to New York City from over 100 miles away.
